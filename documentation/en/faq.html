Frequently Asked Questions

General

What is Websistem?
A lightweight PHP-based web management system focused on clarity and control.
CMS or framework?
Neither by design. It is between raw custom coding and full CMS stacks.
Who is it for?
Freelancers, designers, and developers building small to medium projects.

Installation & Usage

Is installation difficult?
No. Setup is wizard-based and does not require CLI usage.
MySQL or SQLite?
Both are supported.

Themes & Modules

Can I build custom themes?
Yes, themes are HTML-based and fully customizable.
How do modules work?
Embed via shortcode: [al:modul:example_module].

Translation

Is multilingual supported?
Yes, language and translation management are built in.
